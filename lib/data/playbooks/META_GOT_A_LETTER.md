# Understanding Your Letter

## Do This Today

- Call the phone number on the letter (usually at the top or bottom)
- Ask them to explain what the letter means in plain language
- Write down the name of the person you talk to and what they tell you

## Say This

I received a letter from your office and I'm not sure what it means or what I need to do. Can someone help me understand it? The letter says [brief summary if you can read it].

## Bring This

- The letter you received
- Photo ID
- Any other documents related to your case

## If They Stall

- Ask to speak with someone who can explain it clearly
- Request that information be sent in writing if you're confused
- Ask if there's a deadline you need to meet
- Inquire about local offices where you can get help in person

## Local Resources

- Maryland Department of Human Services | 1-800-332-6347 | Find local office at dhs.maryland.gov
- Look for a phone number or office address on the letter itself

## Disclaimers

- Always respond to letters from benefits offices. Ignoring them can result in losing benefits.
- If there's a deadline mentioned, make sure to act before that date.
