# Medicaid MAGI Eligibility Review

## Do This Today

- Complete the review online at MarylandHealthConnection.gov or by phone
- Gather your income documents and household information
- Submit before the deadline to avoid losing coverage

## Say This

I received a letter about my Medicaid MAGI review. I want to complete it so I don't lose my coverage. Can you help me do this now?

## Bring This

- The review letter you received
- Proof of income for everyone in your household (pay stubs, tax returns, etc.)
- Social Security numbers for all household members
- Any changes to your address, phone, or household size

## If They Stall

- Ask for a case manager who can help you complete it immediately
- Request an extension if you need more time to gather documents
- Ask about options if you're having trouble accessing the online system
- Inquire about free local help completing the review

## Local Resources

- Maryland Health Connection | 1-855-642-8572 | marylandhealthconnection.gov
- Local health department offices can provide in-person assistance

## Disclaimers

- Missing the deadline may result in loss of coverage. Complete this as soon as possible.
- You can complete the review online, by phone, or in person at a local office.
