# SNAP Card Not Working

## Do This Today

- Call the EBT customer service line: 1-800-997-2555
- Report the card issue and request a replacement if needed
- Ask about temporary benefits access if you have no food

## Say This

Hello, my EBT card isn't working when I try to use it. Can you help me figure out what's wrong? I need to be able to buy food today.

## Bring This

- Your EBT card
- Your case number if you have it
- Photo ID

## If They Stall

- Ask for a supervisor if the issue can't be resolved
- Request a case number for your call
- Ask about emergency food assistance programs
- Inquire about temporary card access while waiting for replacement

## Local Resources

- Maryland Department of Human Services | 1-800-332-6347 | Find local office at dhs.maryland.gov
- Maryland Hunger Solutions | 410-528-0021

## Disclaimers

- This is general guidance. Your specific situation may require different steps.
- Keep records of all calls including dates, times, and case numbers.
