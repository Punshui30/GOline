# Getting Help With Your Benefits

## Do This Today

- Call your local benefits office or the main hotline
- Explain your situation clearly
- Ask what documents or information you need to bring

## Say This

I need help with my benefits situation, and I'm not sure where to start. Can someone help me understand what I need to do?

## Bring This

- Photo ID
- Any letters or documents you've received
- Proof of income if you have it
- Any cards you've been issued

## If They Stall

- Ask for a case manager or supervisor
- Request a written list of what you need to do
- Inquire about free local help organizations
- Ask about emergency assistance if you have urgent needs

## Local Resources

- Maryland Department of Human Services | 1-800-332-6347 | Find local office at dhs.maryland.gov
- Maryland Hunger Solutions | 410-528-0021
- Community Action Agencies may offer free benefits assistance

## Disclaimers

- Don't give up. Keep calling and asking questions until you get the help you need.
- Many offices have walk-in hours. Consider visiting in person if phone calls aren't working.
