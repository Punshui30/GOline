# Approved But Can't Access Benefits

## Do This Today

- Call the benefits office using the phone number on your approval letter
- Have your case number and approval letter ready
- Ask specifically why you can't access your benefits and what you need to do

## Say This

I received notice that I was approved for benefits, but I can't actually access them. Can you help me figure out what's missing? My case number is [your case number].

## Bring This

- Your approval letter
- Your case number
- Photo ID
- Any cards or documents you received
- The date you were approved

## If They Stall

- Ask to speak with a supervisor or case manager
- Request a written explanation of why you can't access benefits
- Ask for a timeline for when you'll be able to access them
- Inquire about emergency assistance if you have immediate needs

## Local Resources

- Maryland Department of Human Services | 1-800-332-6347 | Find local office at dhs.maryland.gov
- Call the specific program hotline listed on your approval letter

## Disclaimers

- There may be additional steps needed after approval. Follow up until you can access your benefits.
- Keep detailed notes of all conversations including names, dates, and case numbers.
